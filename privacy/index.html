Privacy policy

VolleyTally privacy.

Last reviewed: July 13, 2026. This policy explains how HarpElle LLC handles information related to the VolleyTally app.

Match data and accounts

VolleyTally does not require an account. Team names, match settings, scores, and saved match history are stored locally on your device. VolleyTally does not provide cloud storage or server-side match accounts.

Ads and purchases

Free users may see banner advertisements provided through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad serving, measurement, and fraud prevention under Google’s privacy practices. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed by the applicable app store. RevenueCat helps manage purchase-entitlement status and restore access.

Photos and camera

VolleyTally may request camera or photo-library permission only when you choose to create a post-match photo or add a score frame to a photo. The photo remains on your device unless you choose to share it.

How information is used

Local match information is used to provide scorekeeping, saved matches, and sharing features. Purchase information is used to manage Remove Ads access. Advertising information is handled by the advertising provider as described above.

Your choices

You can control ad personalization through your device settings. You can remove local VolleyTally data through your device’s app settings or by uninstalling the app. You can choose whether to grant photo or camera permissions and whether to share any result.

Children’s privacy

VolleyTally does not require users to provide personal information to use its core scorekeeping features. If you have a privacy concern involving a child, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as VolleyTally changes. The date above will be updated when this page is revised. For privacy questions, contact info@volleytallyapp.com.

Third-party privacy information: Google Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

VolleyTally is a HarpElle LLC product.
