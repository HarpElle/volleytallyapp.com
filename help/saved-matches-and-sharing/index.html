Matches and sharing

Keep the result after the final whistle.

Completed matches are saved on your device, and an in-progress match can be resumed when you come back to the app.

Find a completed match

Open match history to review recent results and the set-by-set score. VolleyTally keeps up to 100 of the most recent completed matches on the device.

Resume an unfinished match

VolleyTally saves active match state locally. Reopen the app to continue from the saved match. Starting a new match replaces the active one.

Share the final

After the match, use the share flow to create a final-score card or add the result to a team photo. Sharing is always your choice and uses your device’s normal sharing options.
