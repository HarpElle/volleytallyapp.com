Correct a score

A stray tap does not have to derail the match.

VolleyTally includes undo and score-correction controls so you can fix the score and stay focused on the next rally.

Undo the last change

Use VolleyTally’s undo control to reverse the most recent scoring change. This is useful when you spot the mistake right away.

Adjust a score

If you need to correct a score after additional rallies, use the app’s score-entry controls to adjust the affected side. Confirm the displayed score before returning to live scoring.

After the final point

If a final point was entered by mistake, correct it before sharing the result. VolleyTally keeps the match state available so you can make the adjustment.
