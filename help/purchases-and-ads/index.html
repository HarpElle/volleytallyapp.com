Purchases and ads

Choose the version that works for you.

VolleyTally is free to use. Free users may see banner ads, and Remove Ads is available as an optional one-time in-app purchase.

Remove Ads

Purchase Remove Ads from within VolleyTally to remove banner ads in normal use. The purchase is handled by the app store on your device.

Restore a purchase

If you purchased Remove Ads before, use the app’s restore-purchase option while signed in to the same app-store account used for the original purchase. An internet connection may be required.

Still need help?

Email info@volleytallyapp.com with your device, app version, and what you tried. Do not include payment information in your message.
